Frequently asked questions

How much does a delivery cost?

Every job is priced on quotation. Send the collection and delivery postcodes, timing and what's being moved using the form, by phone or on WhatsApp, and you'll get a clear price before we set off.

When are you available?

We're available 24/7, including evenings, weekends and bank holidays.

Where do you cover?

We're based in Horbury, Wakefield and mainly work across Wakefield, Leeds and West Yorkshire, with same-day runs anywhere in the UK.

What can you carry?

Our long-wheelbase, high-roof Peugeot Boxer L3H2 takes pallets, boxes, parts and large loads. Tell us the size and weight when you ask for a quote and we'll confirm it fits.

Are you insured?

Yes. We're fully insured, with £5m public liability and £50k goods in transit cover.

Will I get proof of delivery?

Yes. You get proof of delivery when the job is complete, and updates direct from the driver along the way.
